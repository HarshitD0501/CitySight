### Phase D2 Findings
* **Candidate Comparison & Selection:** In the original D2 run, 5-fold `GroupKFold(shuffle=True, random_state=42)` across 144 development wards produced CM pooled WAPE 0.746230, K5 0.624587, and HGB 0.540180. The later corrected export measured HGB 0.540276; HGB still won 4 of 5 folds and remained selected. The small change between runs is unexplained.
* **Challenge Holdout Evaluation, corrected:** Regenerated under the unchanged 12-ward split and selected HGB configuration: WAPE 0.523879, MAE 7.331471, category-mean Spearman 0.767518, mean top-quarter recall 0.611111. Earlier 0.783217/0.333333 values combined categories before ranking; see ISSUE-10.
* **Leakage and Safety:** Verified that altering labels in held-out wards produces identical baseline training predictions; feature medians and zone one-hot encodings are computed strictly from training folds.
